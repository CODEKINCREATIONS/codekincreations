PagePal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 November 2026

PagePal: PDF Book Reader ("PagePal" or "the app") is an Android app published by CodeKin Creations LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: PagePal works entirely offline on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, and we operate no server that receives it.

1. Information We Collect

None. PagePal does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers or location. The app does not have internet access permission, so it cannot send your data to us or to anyone else.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, PagePal keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • PDF files you add: when you add a PDF, a copy is saved inside the app so it can be reopened. Cover thumbnails are generated from the first page.
  • Reading data: the page you are on, reading progress, bookmarks, minutes read per day, your daily goal and streak.
  • Settings: reading mode (Light, Sepia, Night), reminder time and similar preferences.

This information never leaves your device. PagePal does not back it up to the cloud.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show the optional daily reading reminder you set up. Reminders are created on your device; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Run at startup and background work: used by Android's WorkManager so your reminder still arrives after the phone restarts.
  • Files: PagePal only opens PDFs you choose yourself, using Android's system file picker or "Open with PagePal". It cannot browse your other files.

4. Third Parties, Ads and Tracking

PagePal contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can remove a book from your library inside the app at any time. Uninstalling PagePal, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes all of its data, including the copies of your PDFs.

6. Children’s Privacy

PagePal is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the way PagePal handles information changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
