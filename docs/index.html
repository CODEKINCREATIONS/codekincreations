07 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Send us a note and we'll reply within one business day.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted to its scope. After a short discovery call we send a fixed-price proposal with milestones, so you know the full cost before we start.

How long does it take?

Most websites launch in 3–6 weeks. MVP apps and AI tools typically take 6–12 weeks depending on features and integrations.

Do you work with clients outside the U.S.?

We're based in the United States and primarily serve U.S. businesses, but we do take on international projects when time zones allow for good collaboration.

Will I own the code and designs?

Yes. You own 100% of the source code, design files, and accounts once the project is paid in full.

Can you add AI to my existing product?

Absolutely. We regularly integrate AI assistants, smart search, and automations into existing websites, CRMs, and apps without a full rebuild.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of support. After that, many clients move to a monthly Partner retainer for updates, hosting, and new features.
