04 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Send us a note.

What is CodeKin Creations?

We're an independent U.S. software studio. Instead of building for clients, we design, build, and run our own products, and we're working toward turning the strongest ones into startups.

Where can I get your apps?

Write and Calculate run on Windows. PagePal and Expense Tracker run on Android. Use the download buttons in the Products section.

Do you build software for other companies?

No. We've chosen to focus entirely on our own products. We're always happy to hear from potential partners, though.

Are you talking to investors or accelerators?

Yes. We're building toward venture-backed companies and welcome conversations with investors, accelerators, and operators. Reach out through the contact form.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send us a message below and pick "User feedback". We read everything, and user feedback directly shapes what we build next.

How do you handle my data?

We don't sell personal information. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.
